  outline.txt                                 body word count and section list

How it was made
  Project management -> Complete project plan
  Fill the charter fields or load project-record.json
  Save the record, then Draft the plan.

What the plan contains
  Charter, scope, WBS, CPM schedule, cost roll-up, resources, RACI matrix,
  risk register, governance, monitoring and references.

What it does not do
  It does not invent a cost, a duration, an owner or a date. The work-package
  costs sum to USD 480,000 and the declared budget is USD 480,000. The CPM
  durations and the RACI assignments are the sample record's own values.

Word count
  The body count is prose only; WBS, schedule, cost, RACI and risk tables are
  not body words. The 600-word target is a demonstration target, not a rubric.
  The plan is table-heavy, so the prose is shorter than the target. This is
  reported honestly, never padded.

  The table-heavy body is why Word's own count is higher: Word counts table
  cells, while the product's body rule does not.
