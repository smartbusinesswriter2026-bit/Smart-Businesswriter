How a paper enters the pipeline

A paper is not an input. It is a source that must be read, located, reconciled and confirmed before anything it says may shape an analysis.

Structured files take a different path
A spreadsheet is not a paper. FY2025_Management_Accounts.xlsx skips the extraction desk entirely: the profiler reads it directly, and a value in a cell is already located. The cost of the distrust-ingestion controls is paid only where the reading is uncertain — which is exactly why the manual still leaves open whether uploads are structured files only, or papers as well §16.

Intended use
Compare two to five alternatives you define, each grounded in case facts you supply, by net present value at your discount rate, and see where the ranking changes across your rate range. A preference is stated only under a decision rule you set.
Not supported
Choosing alternatives, facts, cash flows, rates or weights for you; qualitative or multi-criteria judgments; risk or probability weighting; unequal-length or mutually exclusive project adjustments beyond what NPV captures.
Limitations
  • NPV treats cash flows as certain and ignores unquantified factors.
  • Alternatives, facts and cash flows are yours; the system checks structure and arithmetic, not whether they are realistic.

Mode switching changes the bill, not the answer. A learned mode policy may change how long a proposal is and when it appears, but the stage order, the frozen test plan, the thresholds and the pass/fail decision stay in code. The gate is a metamorphic test: the same run with latent mode on and with it off must produce an identical spec hash, identical check results and a byte-identical report. Not a security boundary. The guards are best-effort patches of socket, open, os and subprocess. Code using low-level calls could bypass them. On Windows only the timeout applies, and the Windows path is untested. Do not run untrusted or model-written code on this layer.

Tier T2 · custom analysis; expert review pending · claim ceiling: descriptive + prescriptive under a user-set decision rule · currency PHP · period cross_section

This case comparison covers 3 alternatives you supplied, each citing the case facts it follows from (4 facts in total). Net present value is computed for each alternative from the cash flows you supplied, at a discount rate you set of 12.0% and across a range you set from 8.0% to 18.0%.

At your base rate, A3 has the highest net present value of the three alternatives. Under your decision rule highest_npv, A3 ranks first — but see the flip below before treating that as a conclusion.

Where the ranking changes

The ranking of A2 and A3 changes at a discount rate of 9.6%, inside the range you declared. Below that rate A2 ranks first; at and above it A3 ranks first. A1 ranks last across the whole range — it never flips, so it belongs in the appendix rather than the headline.

Flippers belong in the headline, non-flippers in the appendix. The crossover is a property of the cash flows you typed, not a forecast.

What this paper does not do

It does not assess whether your cash flows are realistic. It does not weight risk or probability. It does not compare unequal-length alternatives. It states a preference only because you set the decision rule; change the rule and the conclusion changes with it.

Claim licensing

each claim worded no more strongly than its evidence allows
descriptiveholds
“The regional bakery segment was ₱186.4 million in 2025.”
Earns: data reconciliation — the value was read twice from the source and both readers agreed, and the paper's own line items foot to the total.
associationalworded down
“A supermarket bakery counter is associated with falling bakery sales in the central district.”
Wanted: causal. It would need an identification strategy, its diagnostics and a confounding-sensitivity analysis. The paper supplies none of these, so the draft was written down rather than refused.
prescriptiveholds
“Under your decision rule, A3 ranks first at a 12.0% discount rate.”
Earns: everything above, plus your decision rule and your risk tolerance — both user-owned, neither chosen by the model.
blocked
“Expanding to the coastal road will return 14%.”
Rejected at the drafting scan: a predictive claim needs an out-of-sample backtest beating a naive baseline. There is none, so the sentence may not be written.

What still has to be decided about papers

openmanual §16
“Meaning of ‘papers’… users are students, so papers are most likely coursework, theses or dissertations, which follow institutional conventions — citation style, AI-use disclosure. Confirm which output types are in version 1.”
This mockup assumes both directions: papers in, papers out. If the answer is coursework only, the output side needs citation styles and a disclosure block, and the input side can assume instructor case packs.
openmanual §16
“Will users upload structured files only, or also PDFs, scans and photos? This determines the weight of ingestion controls.”
If structured files only, the extraction desk can be dropped entirely and this whole UI shrinks to the profiler.
openmanual §15B
Security and hostile content. Uploaded files may carry hidden instructions. Document content is data, never instructions.
The intake view shows a content scan, but the requirement — tenant isolation, encryption, retention and deletion rules — has no owner yet.
openmanual §15B
Academic integrity and AI-use policy. Students’ institutions may require disclosure of what the tool did.
View 05 already carries a disclosure-ready summary as a delivery item, because the paper that leaves this pipeline may be submitted.

Governance, in force
The model never produces a number. It produces a problem spec and code. Figures in prose are injected from computed artifacts, and raw numeric literals in model output are rejected.
Correctness has three layers. The pipeline guarantees computational correctness, surfaces formulation problems, and flags input problems. A number derived correctly from a bad input still reads as a hallucination — so key inputs sit next to every headline figure.
