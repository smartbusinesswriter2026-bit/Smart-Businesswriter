## 1. Overview

Kurt Lewin is widely regarded as the founder of social psychology and a pioneer of organizational change theory. His two enduring contributions to people & organization strategy & design are:

1. **Force Field Analysis** — a tool for diagnosing the driving and restraining forces around any proposed change.
2. **The 3-Step Change Model** — Unfreeze → Change (Move) → Refreeze — a sequenced model for implementing organizational change.

This worksheet walks you through applying both frameworks to a real people/organization design challenge.
