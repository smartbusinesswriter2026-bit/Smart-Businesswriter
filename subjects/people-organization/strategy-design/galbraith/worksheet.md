## 1. Overview

Jay Galbraith's Star Model is one of the most influential frameworks in organizational design. It treats an organization as five interlocking design dimensions — a "star" — where each point must be aligned with the others and with the organization's strategy:

1. **Strategy** — direction, objectives, competitive positioning
2. **Structure** — where decision rights sit, departmentalization, reporting lines
3. **Processes** — information flow, lateral coordination, decision processes
4. **Rewards** — goals, metrics, incentives, recognition
5. **People** — recruitment, development, role fit, talent management

**The core insight:** changing one star point without aligning the others creates misalignment and failure.
