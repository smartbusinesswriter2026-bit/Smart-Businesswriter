Deliberately not here

  • No sign-in, password, e-mail address or sign-out. There is no identity to authenticate and no profile to log out of.
  • No card details. A subscription is bought on the pricing page and paid inside PayPal; this server never sees a card number and stores no payment instrument.
  • No credits to buy separately. The wallet above is a local ledger that meters exports on this machine, granted on first use — not a currency you purchase. What a subscription grants is still to be confirmed.
  • No cloud storage, no sync, no cross-device access. Every case, chat, draft and ledger entry stays in files on this machine.
  • No analytics, tag manager, tracker or third-party script. This page loads no remote font, no tag and no remote asset, and reads only this machine's own API.
  • No support inbox. There is one user, and it is the person at this keyboard.
