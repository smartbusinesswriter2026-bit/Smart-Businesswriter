Check my trial balance

Paste your trial balance straight from Excel: one account per line, with its debit or credit. Nothing is calculated on this page — the engine compares what you give it with the trial balance your own journal produces, and tells you where the two disagree.

With only a trial balance I can check that it balances and is internally consistent. That is the weakest check in accounting: errors of principle, commission, original entry, omission, reversal and compensating errors all leave a trial balance balanced.

Help & about

What this is

A local workbench that calls the local API. Every number shown is computed by the method library and printed exactly as the server returned it. Nothing is calculated in the browser.

How it works

Pick what you need from the rail. A subject page holds that subject's tiles; a tile is either a tool you fill in or a workspace with its own instructions. Describe the case in the box on Home and press the arrow if you want the guided paper instead: the chat asks only what it still needs, proposes a plan you confirm, then finds the evidence and drafts the section.
Figures come from the subject rail or the Business Calculator: pick a subject, enter your numbers, and the method library computes the answer with its checks, its limits and an export you can submit.
Every number is computed by the method library and printed exactly as the server returned it. Nothing is calculated in the browser.

Which one do I need?

Financial statements to prepare the statements and the working from a trial balance. Subjects for one topic and its tools, such as break-even or a ratio. Business Calculator for one calculation. Check for a trial balance against your journal. Business plan for the plan sections and financial blocks. Portfolio for a dated valuation of your holdings and prices. Guided paper for a full PESTEL, SWOT or combined paper with sources.

Every subject page has a Student guide with a Start here path, what to prepare and what the shelf does not do.

Key words

  • Calculation — one method with figures you declare.
  • Worked example — a preset you replace with your own numbers.
  • Checks — the engine's own verification; a failed check blocks the result.
  • T1 / T2 — a standard tool or a custom analysis; the result says which.
  • Blocked — the engine refused; the reason is shown, and nothing is guessed.
