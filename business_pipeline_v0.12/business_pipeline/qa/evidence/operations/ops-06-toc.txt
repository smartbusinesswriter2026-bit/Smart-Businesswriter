Theory of Constraints
The theory of constraints treats one system at a time: the step that limits output is identified, exploited, protected by the pacing of everything else, elevated if it still binds, and reassessed once it moves. Each step is treated in turn, first through the evidence accepted for the case and then through what that evidence implies for the decision.
