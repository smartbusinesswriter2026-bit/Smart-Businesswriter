Demand is treated as level over the year and the lead time is constant; seasonality, quantity discounts and supplier reliability are not modelled. Safety stock is the declared service level times the demand standard deviation over the square root of the lead time, using the normal approximation. The total annual cost counts ordering and holding cost only: no purchase cost, stock-out cost, transport or warehouse space cost.
