An association between the row and column variables does not show that one causes the other. The test treats the counts as independent observations of a single sample. No power or minimum-detectable-effect calculation is reported; the confidence interval shows the precision the sample actually achieved. Survey weights, clustered observations and repeated measures are not supported; every observation is treated as independent.
