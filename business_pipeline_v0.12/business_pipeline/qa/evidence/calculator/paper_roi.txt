Computation: return on investment
Result
A net gain of 125,000.00 on an investment of 500,000.00 gives a return on investment of 25.00%. Compounded over 3.00 years, the annualised return is 7.72%. The net gain is the total gain you declared over the whole holding period, net of the investment; it is not an annual cash flow. The annualised return is the constant yearly rate that compounds to the total return; it is not a forecast and it ignores the timing of the flows. The method does not compute tax, depreciation or the cost of capital; use the appraisal tools for a discounted decision.

Limitations
