Computation: campaign efficiency
Result
The campaign recorded 250,000 impressions and 6,000 clicks, a click-through rate of 2.40%, at 10.00 per click and 240.00 per thousand impressions. It produced 220 conversions at 272.73 each, on a conversion rate of 3.67%. Revenue was 150,000.00, so return on ad spend is 2.50 and advertising cost of sales is 40.00%. At the declared contribution margin, break-even return on ad spend is 2.22 and profit after media spend is 7,500.00. Every figure is arithmetic on the counts you declare; the method does not read an ad platform or reconcile its numbers. A conversion is whatever you declared it to be; the rate is not comparable with another campaign that defined it differently. Revenue is attributed to the campaign as you supplied it. No incrementality, overlap or multi-channel effect is modelled.

Assumptions made
Impressions, clicks, conversions and revenue cover the same campaign and period.
A conversion is the action you declared, counted once per acquisition.
Limitations
Arithmetic on declared counts; no platform read, model or attribution window.
No incrementality, overlap, view-through or assisted-conversion adjustment.
Revenue and spend must share a currency and a period.
How the figures were checked
Second implementation agrees.
Ctr is clicks over impressions.
Cpa is spend over conversions.
Roas is the inverse of acos.
Cpc is cpa times conversion rate.
