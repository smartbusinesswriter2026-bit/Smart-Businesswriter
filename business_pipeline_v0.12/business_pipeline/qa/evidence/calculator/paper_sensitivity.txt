Computation: sensitivity table
Result
At the declared base case the profit is 350,000.00; the table reports profit for each declared price and volume change. The table changes price and volume only; cost changes need a separate table or a declared second run. Each cell is profit at the same declared unit cost and fixed cost; no demand response to price is modelled. A table is arithmetic on declared changes, not a forecast or a probability. Missing values (blank entries and declared missing codes) are excluded listwise before the computation; the missing-data mechanism (MCAR, MAR or MNAR) was not established, so the results assume the excluded values are not systematically different.
