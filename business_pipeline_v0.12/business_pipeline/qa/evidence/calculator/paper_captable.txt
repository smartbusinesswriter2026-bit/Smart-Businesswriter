Computation: cap table round
Result
At a pre-money valuation of 4,000,000.00 and an investment of 1,000,000.00, the post-money valuation is 5,000,000.00 at 4.00 a share. The investor receives 250,000.00 shares, or 18.00% of the company, and the founders keep 72.00%. The declared option pool takes 10.00% of the fully diluted company. One round only: the pre-money value, the investment and the founders' shares are declared, and no earlier round, convertible note or SAFE is modelled. The option pool is a declared share of the fully diluted post-money company; a pool created before the money (a pool shuffle that lowers the price) is a different convention and is not applied. No liquidation preference, anti-dilution, vesting, pro-rata right, board seat or term-sheet clause is modelled; those are negotiated terms, not arithmetic.
