Computation: discounted payback
Result
The discounted cash flows recover the initial outlay after 3.02 periods (during period 4), and the net present value over the whole horizon is 267.95. Discounted payback is a liquidity measure: it ignores every cash flow after the recovery point.

What was computed

Assumptions made
Cash flows occur at period ends; the recovering period's flow is spread evenly.
Limitations
Ignores every cash flow after the payback point, so it measures liquidity, not value.
Within-period interpolation is a declared convention, not a data fact.
A higher rate can push recovery beyond the horizon, which blocks instead of reporting 'never within the horizon' as a number.
How the figures were checked
Second implementation agrees.
Zero rate equals undiscounted payback.
A higher rate cannot shorten payback.
Scaling the cash flows leaves payback unchanged.
Unrecovered outlays are refused.
