Computation: tariff welfare
Result
The tariff raises the domestic price to 15.00, with imports of 20.00 and tariff revenue of 100.00. The total deadweight loss is 50.00. The country is a small price-taker in world markets; the tariff is fully passed into the domestic price. The deadweight loss is the textbook production and consumption triangles for a linear partial-equilibrium model. No retaliation, terms-of-trade effect, quota, transport cost or general-equilibrium effect is modelled.
