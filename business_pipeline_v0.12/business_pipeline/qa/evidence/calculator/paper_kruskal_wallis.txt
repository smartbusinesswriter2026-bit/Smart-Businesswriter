Result
The analysis compares 3 groups. Group a has 3 valid observations with a median of 2.000; Group b has 3 valid observations with a median of 5.000; Group c has 3 valid observations with a median of 8.000; 0 entries were excluded as missing. Kruskal-Wallis gives H = 7.200 with a p-value of 0.0273. At the declared significance level of 5.00% the rank distributions are statistically significantly different. Caution: the sample is small, so these estimates are imprecise and should be described, not generalized. Kruskal-Wallis compares rank distributions, not means; a difference does not show that group membership produces it, and no causal claim is made. The p-value uses a chi-square approximation that is less accurate for small groups. Missing values (blank entries and declared missing codes) are excluded listwise before the computation; the missing-data mechanism (MCAR, MAR or MNAR) was not established, so the results assume the excluded values are not systematically different. One omnibus hypothesis is tested and no post-hoc pairwise comparisons are made, so no multiple-comparison correction is applied. No power or minimum-detectable-effect calculation is reported; the confidence interval shows the precision the sample actually achieved. Survey weights, clustered observations and repeated measures are not supported; every observation is treated as independent.
