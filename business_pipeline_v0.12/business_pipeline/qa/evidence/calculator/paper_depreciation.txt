Computation: depreciation schedule
Result
Depreciation on a cost of 500,000.00 to a salvage value of 50,000.00 over 5 periods totals 450,000.00, leaving a carrying amount of 50,000.00. The schedule is a declared accounting allocation, not a tax depreciation rule or a fair-value estimate. Straight-line divides the depreciable amount evenly; declining balance applies the declared rate to the carrying amount and plugs the final period to salvage. No disposals, revaluations, impairment or mid-year conventions are modelled.
