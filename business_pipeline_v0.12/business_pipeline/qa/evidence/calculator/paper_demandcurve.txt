The fit is an association between the prices and quantities you observed, not an identified demand curve; omitted variables, simultaneity and measurement error are not addressed. A line is imposed on the data; a non-linear or kinked demand pattern will not be captured. The revenue-maximising price exists only when the fitted slope is negative, and it is arithmetic on the fit, not a pricing recommendation. Missing values (blank entries and declared missing codes) are excluded listwise before the computation; the missing-data mechanism (MCAR, MAR or MNAR) was not established, so the results assume the excluded values are not systematically different. One hypothesis is tested, so no multiple-comparison correction is applied. No power or minimum-detectable-effect calculation is reported; the confidence interval shows the precision the sample actually achieved. Survey weights, clustered observations and repeated measures are not supported; every observation is treated as independent.
