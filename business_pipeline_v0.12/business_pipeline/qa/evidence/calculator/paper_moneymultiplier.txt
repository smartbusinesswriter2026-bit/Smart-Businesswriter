Computation: money multiplier
Result
With a monetary base of 100.00 and a reserve ratio of 0.1000, the simple money multiplier is 10.0000 and the currency-adjusted multiplier is 7.0000. The implied money supply is 700.00. The money multiplier is a declared reserve-ratio identity, not a forecast of the money supply. The simple multiplier assumes no currency drain and no excess reserves; the extended multiplier adds a declared currency drain but still assumes no excess reserves. Banks are assumed to lend to the reserve requirement; the model does not include capital, liquidity or demand constraints.
