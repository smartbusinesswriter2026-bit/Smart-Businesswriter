Computation: cash runway
Result
With 300,000.00 of opening cash and a monthly net cash flow of -40,000.00, the runway is 7.50 months. The lowest balance within 9 months is -60,000.00 and the funding gap over that horizon is 60,000.00. Runway is arithmetic on the opening cash and the monthly net flow you declared; it is not a forecast of sales or costs. Growth is applied to the declared net flow, so revenue and costs are assumed to move together; a different cost path needs its own run. The funding gap is the cash needed to keep the running balance at zero through the declared target month; it ignores financing costs.

Limitations
