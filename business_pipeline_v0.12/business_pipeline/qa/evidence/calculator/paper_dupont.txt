DuPont analysis is arithmetic on one period's reported figures; average balances and adjustments are not applied, and a comparison period is used exactly as declared. Growth is a simple relative change against one declared prior period, not a compound growth rate or a trend line.

Assumptions made
The figures are one period on one accounting basis and in one currency.
Equity is positive, so the return is interpretable.

Limitations
Descriptive arithmetic only; no benchmark, threshold or verdict.
No average balances; one declared comparison period only, not a multi-year trend.
A negative equity makes ROE uninterpretable and is refused.
