Result

Only the discount rate changes between the scenarios. The two scenarios change only the discount rate; the cash flows are held fixed, so this is not a full scenario analysis of revenue, cost and timing. The break-even rate is the single internal rate of return of the declared cash flows; when the flows change sign more than once no single break-even rate is reported. A range around one input says nothing about the joint effect of several inputs changing together; a simulation or a full scenario model is a different method.
