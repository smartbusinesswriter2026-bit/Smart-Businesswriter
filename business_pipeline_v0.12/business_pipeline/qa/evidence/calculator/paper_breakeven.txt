Computation: breakeven
Result
At a contribution margin of 10.00 per unit, the business breaks even at 100.00 units, or 2,000.00 of sales. Break-even assumes constant unit price, constant variable cost and constant fixed cost over the relevant range. The margin of safety is measured against the expected volume you declared, not against a forecast the method verifies. A target profit is a pre-tax profit target in the same currency as the costs; tax is not computed.
