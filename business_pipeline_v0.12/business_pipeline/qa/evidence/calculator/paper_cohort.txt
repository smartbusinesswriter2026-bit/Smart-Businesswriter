Computation: cohort retention
Result
Across the declared cohorts, first-period retention is 100.00% and the average retention over the curve is 68.16%, giving an average lifetime of 2.73 periods. Retention is pooled across the cohorts you declare; each period is the count you supply over the cohort's starting size. Cohorts must be observed for the same number of periods; a period a cohort has not reached should be left out rather than entered as zero. No survival model, seasonality, reactivation or revenue weighting is applied; the average lifetime is the area under the declared retention curve. Missing values (blank entries and declared missing codes) are excluded listwise before the computation; the missing-data mechanism (MCAR, MAR or MNAR) was not established, so the results assume the excluded values are not systematically different.
