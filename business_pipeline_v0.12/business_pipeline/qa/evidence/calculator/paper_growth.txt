The CAGR is the constant rate that compounds the start value to the end value over the declared number of periods; it is not a forecast. The rule of 72 is an approximation; the exact doubling time is ln(2)/ln(1 + CAGR). No inflation adjustment is made unless the values you supplied are already real.
