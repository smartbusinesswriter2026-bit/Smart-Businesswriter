Computation: pricing decision
Result
At a unit cost of 15.00 and a declared markup of 60.00%, the cost-plus price is 24.00. At the declared volume the break-even price is 22.50. A target margin of 37.50% on the price gives 24.00. To earn the declared target profit, the price must be 25.83. Every price is arithmetic on the declared unit cost, fixed cost, volume and rate; the method does not research what customers will pay. Cost-plus marks up the cost; the target-margin price divides by one minus the margin on price. The two rules give different prices and the report shows both. No competitor price, willingness-to-pay estimate or demand response is used; price elasticity is a separate method.
