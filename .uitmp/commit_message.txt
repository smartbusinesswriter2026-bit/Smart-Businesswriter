fix(web): transparent logo, restored favicon, larger hero mark

The Home logo no longer arrives on a white card. The owner's PNG is RGB with
no alpha channel - the mark was flattened onto solid #FCFCFC paper, so the
copy in the page carried that paper with it and sat on a white rectangle over
the hero wash. web/assets/mark.png is now cut from web/assets/logo.png with a
real alpha channel (paper outside the artwork transparent, paper inside the
book opaque), written from the same bytes the page inlines.

The favicon is back. Commit 977c1cc had pasted a scaffold document in front of
the real page, so a browser parsed the scaffold as the document and the four
icon links landed in the body, where no browser reads a favicon from. The page
is one document again and the 16/32/64/apple-touch icons are in the head.

The hero mark is 20% larger (78px -> 94px, 56px -> 67px under 900px), sits 6px
lower and closes to a 6px gap above "Smart Business Writer", so the two read
as one lockup. The topbar mark and the chat button keep their sizes.

New scripts/generate_mark.py rebuilds the mark and re-inlines it, reusing the
alpha cut from scripts/generate_favicons.py. New
tests/test_web_brand_assets.py (5 tests, standard library only) holds all of
it: one document, the icon links in the head, the logo's corner transparent
and its artwork present, the asset identical to the inline copy, and no
opaque corner in any favicon.

No computation, route contract, frozen fixture or business number changed.
