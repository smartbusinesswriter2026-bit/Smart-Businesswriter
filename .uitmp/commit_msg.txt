Design: the collapsed rail shows its icons in full, and two Home copy edits

The three design increments of this morning are in the tree already - a concurrent
lane's commits swept them in while their copy of the tree was in flight. This
commit closes the record: it restores the worklog entry that lane's commit of
docs/WORKLOG.md had overwritten, and records the rail geometry as B-165.

What the three increments were:

- The collapsed rail shows its icons in full (web/index.html, section 17 of the
  design notes). The subject list scrolls inside the rail and a vertical
  scrollbar takes the scrollport's right edge, so with the 45 px strip the
  scrollport ended at x = 30 while every glyph sits at x = 18.5 -> 37.5 - under
  the scrollbar's column and cut in half. The collapsed rail is now the strip
  plus a column for the scrollbar: --rail-sb:16px and
  --rail-w-collapsed:calc(var(--rail-w) + var(--rail-sb)), applied by
  body:not(.rail-open) .rail{width:var(--rail-w-collapsed)} - 61 px in the wide
  layout, 60 px in the narrow one. The green strip keeps its own width, so the
  open rail is untouched, and the icons do not move: the glyph is still at
  18.5 -> 37.5. scripts/ui_smoke.py carries the geometry markers so the column
  cannot be silently dropped.
- The Home hero drops the "BUSINESS | ACCOUNTING | ECONOMICS | FINANCE"
  tagline (section 20, B-162): removed from the static markup and from the
  RENDERERS.home rebuild, with the CSS that only served it.
- The Home slogan loses the full stop after "assistant" (section 22, B-164):
  one character, in the same two places, with the three proofs and their own
  full stops untouched.
