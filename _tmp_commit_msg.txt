Corpus: DATABASE.md, the design record and the build tools; the PDF library stays out of git

DATABASE.md is the step-by-step build manual for the corpus database and
corpus-db/CORPUS_DB.md is the design authority behind it. Both are now in git,
with the build tools they drive: the eight-table schema, the shared library,
the ingest tool, the enrichment worker, the admin service and page, the
Meilisearch settings and the regression tests.

The owner's PDF library (DATABASE/, 294 files, 1.7 GB) is a working collection,
not source, and stays out of git. .gitignore now keeps it out, and keeps the
corpus runtime state out with it - corpus.sqlite, pdfs/ and meili/ - because
the database is rebuilt from the PDFs and the search index is disposable. The
catalogue that describes the library (manifest.csv, sources.bib, README.txt) is
committed under corpus-db/catalogue/ so the corpus can be rebuilt from the
record rather than from the bytes.

Also in this commit: AGENTS.md points at the manual, the design record and the
owner walkthrough; CONDUCTOR_MANUAL.md carries the layered source order and the
corpus gate rules (B-168, B-169); DECISIONS.md, CHANGELOG.md and WORKLOG.md
record those decisions and the manual builder (B-171); CONTINUATION_NOTE.md
keeps D-17, D-18 and Q-19; scripts/build_manual_docx.py and its test build the
Word copies of the manual and the walkthrough.
