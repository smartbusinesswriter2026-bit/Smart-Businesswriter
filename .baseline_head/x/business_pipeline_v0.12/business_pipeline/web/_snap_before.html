Check my trial balance

Paste your trial balance straight from Excel: one account per line, with its debit or credit. Nothing is calculated on this page — the engine compares what you give it with the trial balance your own journal produces, and tells you where the two disagree.

With only a trial balance I can check that it balances and is internally consistent. That is the weakest check in accounting: errors of principle, commission, original entry, omission, reversal and compensating errors all leave a trial balance balanced.

Help & about

What this is

A local workbench that calls the local API. Every number shown is computed by the method library and printed exactly as the server returned it. Nothing is calculated in the browser.

How to work

1. Describe the question on Home and run it, or pick a subject in the rail.
2. Read the work plan and the frozen checks, then confirm.
3. Read the result with its honesty notes and limitations.
4. Save it as a case; cases keep versions, comparisons and sign-offs.

Offline

No external network calls, no web fonts, no analytics.
