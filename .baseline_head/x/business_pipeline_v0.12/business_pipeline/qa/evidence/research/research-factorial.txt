Computation: two way anova
Result
The balanced two-way ANOVA over 8 observations has an F of 4.000 for the first factor with a p-value of 0.1161, an F of 16.000 for the second factor with a p-value of 0.0161, and an interaction F of 0.000 with a p-value of 1.0000. The decomposition is reported in the appendix; no post-hoc comparison is implied by an omnibus test. Balanced two-way ANOVA only: unequal cell counts are refused because the sums of squares would depend on a type-of-SS convention the manual does not fix. A significant interaction means the simple effect of one factor depends on the level of the other; the omnibus tests alone do not say where the difference lies (use the post-hoc pairwise method for the groups you intended). Assumptions (normality, equal variances, independence) are not tested; the design and the cell sizes must be checked by the person who collected the data. One hypothesis is tested, so no multiple-comparison correction is applied. No power or minimum-detectable-effect calculation is reported; the confidence interval shows the precision the sample actually achieved. Survey weights, clustered observations and repeated measures are not supported; every observation is treated as independent.
