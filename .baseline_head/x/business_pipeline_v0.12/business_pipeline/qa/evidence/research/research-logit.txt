Computation: logistic regression
Result
The logistic model over 40 observations converged in 6 iterations with a McFadden pseudo R-squared of 0.1528. Coefficients are log-odds and odds ratios are reported per predictor; a coefficient is not an effect size and no causal claim is made. The outcome must be coded 0/1; the model estimates log-odds, so a coefficient is not a probability or an effect size. No regularisation is applied; complete separation or a collinear design blocks with an error instead of reporting an unstable fit. Standard errors are the usual large-sample Wald ones; small samples and rare outcomes make them optimistic, and no goodness-of-fit test is run. Missing values (blank entries and declared missing codes) are excluded listwise before the computation; the missing-data mechanism (MCAR, MAR or MNAR) was not established, so the results assume the excluded values are not systematically different. One hypothesis is tested, so no multiple-comparison correction is applied. No power or minimum-detectable-effect calculation is reported; the confidence interval shows the precision the sample actually achieved. Survey weights, clustered observations and repeated measures are not supported; every observation is treated as independent.
