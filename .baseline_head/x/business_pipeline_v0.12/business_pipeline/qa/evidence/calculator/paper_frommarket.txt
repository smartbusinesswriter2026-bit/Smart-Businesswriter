Computation: sales from market
Result
A declared 3.00% share of a 60,000,000.00 market gives revenue of 1,800,000.00, or 15,000.00 units at 120.00. The market size and the share are declared; the method does not estimate either. Revenue assumes the whole target share is sold at the declared price; no ramp-up, discount or seasonality. A share is a plan assumption, not evidence that customers will buy.
