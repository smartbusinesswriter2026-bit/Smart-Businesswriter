The model is a multi-server queue with random arrivals and service times (M/M/c), with constant average rates and interchangeable servers. Arrivals must be below the number of servers times the service rate; at or above it the queue never clears and the method blocks. It ignores priorities, abandonment, balking, batch arrivals, server heterogeneity and any distribution other than exponential.

Assumptions made
Arrivals are random and independent at a constant average rate.
Service times are exponential with a constant average rate per server.
Servers are interchangeable, with one queue and no priority or abandonment.
Limitations
No priorities, abandonment, batch arrivals or heterogeneous servers.
The exponential assumption can understate or overstate real queues.
At or above servers x service rate the queue never clears and the method blocks.
