Result
Across 1,000 seeded simulations the mean duration is 7.71, with median 7.68, eightieth percentile 8.46 and ninetieth percentile 8.86. The share of runs within the target is 0.9290. Durations are independent triangular draws between the optimistic and pessimistic values with the most likely as the mode; that distribution is a declared convention. The simulation is seeded and reproducible, but a different seed gives different percentiles; it is not a forecast or a guarantee. Resource limits, calendars and correlated durations are not modelled; the criticality index is the share of runs in which an activity had zero float.
