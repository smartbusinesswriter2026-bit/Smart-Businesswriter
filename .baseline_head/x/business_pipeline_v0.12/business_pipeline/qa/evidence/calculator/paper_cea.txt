Computation: cost effectiveness
Result
Compared with the first-listed option, the analysis reports 2 options with an incremental cost per unit of effect for each non-comparator. The ratios are in the appendix; an option that costs less and is more effective is flagged as dominant rather than given a ratio. An incremental cost-effectiveness ratio compares each option with the first-listed comparator only; it says nothing about options against each other unless one is the baseline. The ratio is undefined when the effect does not change; a dominant option (cheaper and more effective) needs no ratio, and the flag reports it. Effects must be on one declared outcome scale; the system does not convert or weight different outcomes.

Limitations

An option with the same effect as the comparator has an undefined ratio and blocks.
Dominance is flagged, not ranked.
