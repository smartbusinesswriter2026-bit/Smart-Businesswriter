Computation: market power
Result
At a price of 50.00 and a marginal cost of 20.00, the markup is 30.00 and the Lerner index is 0.6000. The implied demand elasticity is 1.6667. The Lerner index is (price - marginal cost) / price, computed from the two figures you declared. It is a static markup measure, not proof of market power or of harm to consumers. The implied elasticity is the inverse of the Lerner index only under the declared single-price monopoly model.
