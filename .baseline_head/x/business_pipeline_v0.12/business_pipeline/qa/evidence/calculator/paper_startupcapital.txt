Computation: startup capital
Result
The set-up costs of 350,000.00 plus working capital of 120,000.00 give a contingency reserve of 47,000.00 and a total funding requirement of 517,000.00. Against declared sources of 350,000.00, the amount to raise is 167,000.00. The start-up costs and working capital are the figures you declared; the method does not price equipment, rent or inventory. The contingency is a declared percentage of the set-up costs plus working capital, not a risk assessment. When the declared sources exceed the requirement the surplus is reported separately; the amount to raise is never shown as negative.
