Computation: sales funnel
Result
From 10,000 at the first stage to 500 customers at the last, the overall conversion is 5.00%, with 9,500 leaving the funnel along the way. At the declared spend, the acquisition cost is 50.00 per customer. The stages are the ones you declare, in the order you declare; the method does not decide the funnel or the conversion points. Counts must not increase from one stage to the next; a rise means the stage order or the counting is wrong. No cohort, time period, attribution or channel split is modelled; a conversion rate is arithmetic on the two counts you supply.

Assumptions made

Limitations
Arithmetic on declared counts; no estimation, attribution or significance.
No cohort, channel or time split.
A rising count blocks rather than being smoothed.
