Computation: purchase intent
Result
Across 400 respondents, the declared weights give an intent rate of 33.75%. Applied to the declared population, that is 6,750 projected buyers and 1,687,500.00 of projected revenue. The conversion weights are declared conventions, not estimates; a different set of weights gives a different intent rate. Stated intent is not a purchase. The projection assumes the respondents are representative of the population you declared. No survey design, sampling error, non-response or seasonality is modelled; the counts and the population are yours.
